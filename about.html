Our Story

A Decade of Compassionate Care

Founded in 2010, Riverwalk Medical Centre began with a simple vision: to create a healthcare experience that treats every patient like family. What started as a small practice with two doctors has grown into a multidisciplinary medical centre serving over 50,000 patients annually.

Our philosophy is rooted in the belief that healthcare should be accessible, continuous, and compassionate. We don't just treat symptoms - we build lasting relationships with our patients and their families.
